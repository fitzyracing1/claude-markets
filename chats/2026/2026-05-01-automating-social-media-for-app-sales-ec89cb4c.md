Here are the most effective ways to automate social media for app sales:

**Content Scheduling**
- Use tools like **Buffer, Hootsuite, or Later** to schedule posts across platforms in advance. Write a week's worth of content in one sitting and let it drip out automatically.

**Auto-post from RSS/triggers**
- Connect your blog or app update feed to social media using **Zapier or Make (formerly Integromat)**. New blog post → auto-tweet, for example.

**AI-generated content pipelines**
- Use an AI tool (or build one with Claude's API) to generate varied post copy — feature highlights, testimonials, tips — then schedule them. This keeps content fresh without manual effort.

**Paid ad automation**
- Run **Meta or Google Ads** with automated rules (e.g., increase budget when ROAS is above X, pause when cost-per-install exceeds Y). Both platforms have built-in automation.

**Engagement automation** (use carefully)
- Tools like **ManyChat** can auto-reply to DMs or comments with a link to your app — great for Instagram and Facebook funnels.

**Review & testimonial reposts**
- Set up a Zapier flow: when someone leaves a 5-star App Store review → auto-generate a shareable graphic and queue it for posting.

**Analytics-driven scheduling**
- Tools like **Sprout Social** identify when your audience is most active and auto-publish at optimal times.

---

**Recommended starting stack for an indie app:**
1. **Make or Zapier** — backbone for connecting everything
2. **Buffer** — scheduling
3. **Claude API** — generating post variations at scale
4. **Meta Ads Manager** — automated paid acquisition

Here's a solid **free-only** stack:

**Scheduling & Posting**
- **Buffer (free tier)** — 3 channels, 10 scheduled posts each. Good enough to start.
- **Meta Business Suite** — free scheduling for Facebook + Instagram natively.

**Automation & Workflows**
- **Zapier (free tier)** — 100 tasks/month, single-step zaps. Enough for basic triggers.
- **Make (free tier)** — 1,000 operations/month, more flexible than Zapier's free plan.

**Content Generation**
- **Claude.ai (free)** — write a week of posts in minutes. Just paste your app description and ask for 10 post variations.

**Link in Bio / App Funnel**
- **Linktree (free)** — one link that routes followers to your App Store page, website, etc.

**Posting to Multiple Platforms at Once**
- **Publer or Planable** both have limited free tiers for multi-platform posting.

---

**Simple free workflow:**
1. Use Claude to generate 2 weeks of posts
2. Schedule them in Buffer or Meta Business Suite
3. Use Make to auto-repost any positive mentions or reviews
4. Drive all traffic to a Linktree → App Store page

The biggest lever is **consistency over tools** — even manually posting 3x/week with good copy beats a complex paid setup.
